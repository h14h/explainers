# Collage

Use a whimsical hand-drawn collage aesthetic: layered paper cutouts, torn edges, pencil doodles, and ink illustrations on a lightly textured paper background. Mix playful handwritten lettering with readable labels, warm colors, and occasional bright accents. Let elements overlap, sit slightly crooked, and feel assembled by hand rather than perfectly aligned.

Animate with playful stop-motion-like movement, cutouts being placed or shuffled, and doodles drawing themselves into the scene. Keep the handmade imperfections intentional and the composition clear; whimsy should support the explanation, not clutter it.
